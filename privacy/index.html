Privacy, without fog.

This pilot collects less data because that is the sensible default.

What this site stores

Nothing on your device. No cookies, and nothing written to your browser's storage, on any page. There is no account, no session and no preference to remember.

Analytics and cookies

Visits are counted by GoatCounter, an analytics service hosted in the European Union. It records which page was opened, where you arrived from, your country, and the kind of browser and device you used.

It does not record your IP address; that is discarded once the visit has been counted. It does not set anything on your device, so it cannot recognise you on a later visit. Counting is done with a hash on the server that changes every day.

One thing is counted beyond page views: opening a route in Google Maps. It is the only way to tell which routes people actually set out on, rather than which ones they read about.

There are no cookies and no advertising pixels. Nothing is stored on your device and nothing is read from it, so there is no consent banner to click.

If you would rather not be counted, turn on Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control in your browser. This site honours both.

Forms

The visible feedback forms are not connected to a processor yet, so this build does not transmit or retain their contents. Before collection is enabled, the form will name the processor, purpose, retention period and contact route.

External links

Links to venues and ticket pages take you to other organisations. Their privacy practices apply once you leave this site.

Route pages also link to Google Maps for directions. Opening one hands you to Google, on Google's terms rather than these.

Contact

This is a private validation pilot and is not yet collecting personal data. A dedicated privacy contact will be added before any public form is switched on.
