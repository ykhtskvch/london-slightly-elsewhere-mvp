Prototype long version: Hillingdon Council documents the six-mile Yellow route from Ruislip to Harefield. This card adds the Lido circuit and woodland paths to make a full day; use the official guide and a live map rather than assuming one continuous signed 15 km line.

We never ask for your location.

The day for when a Tube ride needs to become a reservoir, a long quiet wood and enough space to uncoil.

Start with the Metropolitan line and Ruislip’s old village edge, then let the path become quieter by degrees. The Lido is the pause: water, woodland and coffee if the café is operating. Afterwards the day gets simpler again, following the published woods route towards Harefield. It is a low-stakes walk with a real point-to-point finish, which is rarer than it sounds.

Who it suits, and who it does not.

  • A gentle first full-day walk
  • Friends who want a café pause rather than a pub itinerary
  • Anyone who likes the Metropolitan line but not the idea of going very far

Not ideal for

  • A hard-walking day
  • People who want a direct rail finish
  • A sunny summer afternoon at the Lido if crowds undo the point

Not an isolated wilderness, a swimming day or a route whose exact final bus can be guessed from an old timetable.

On a date

A generous second or third date route: the café stop gives shape, but the walk has enough quiet not to feel scheduled.

With friends

Good for two to four people who agree on a live bus check before the final leg. Keep the Lido pause short on a busy day.

Alone

An approachable solo full-day route. The real decision is whether the long version still feels useful after the Lido; the shorter route is equally valid.

Read this before the platform.

Terrain

Mostly gentle woodland and reservoir paths with a few uneven or muddy stretches. The longer version is assembled from the published Yellow route, a Lido circuit and public woodland paths.

Mud and drainage

Woodland ground becomes muddy after rain. The Lido paths are firmer, but the connecting woods are not a pavement walk.

Exposed sections

The reservoir edge can be windy and bright; the woods provide more shelter but can stay damp after rain.

Road sections

There are short road approaches at Ruislip and Harefield. Use marked crossings and keep the live route open at the transitions.

Stiles

Expect gates and variable path surfaces; do not rely on a fully step-free or mobility-accessible route.

Food and water

The Lido has a café, but carry water and a snack. Café and visitor-attraction hours can vary by season.

Toilets

Use the station or Lido facilities when open. Toilets outside those points should not be assumed.

Mobile signal

Generally usable, though woodland cover can make mapping slower. Download the route information before you leave.

Pub timing

This is a café route, not a pub route. Do not build the timing around a Harefield venue being open.

Shortening

Use the published six-mile Yellow route from Ruislip to Harefield, or make the Lido a shorter out-and-back from Ruislip if you want to keep the day within easy transport.

Before you go.

Best time

A dry weekday or early weekend start

Food and drink

Treat the Lido café as a bonus and carry a snack. Its hours can change, and the route should not collapse if it is closed.

How the day can change.

Published Yellow Route

The Council’s six-mile Ruislip-to-Harefield version without the added Lido circuit.

3–4 hours plus travel · Tube fare + packed food

Full Lido + woods version

The prototype 15 km day: Ruislip village, woods, the Lido circuit and Harefield.

5–6 hours plus travel · Tube fare + café stop

If it rains

Choose the published Yellow Route or a shorter woods-and-Lido circuit. The woodland additions do not improve with deep mud.

If it goes well

Finish in Harefield and take the next practical bus. Do not add a random pub hunt just because the route has ended gently.

If you want to leave early

At the Lido, use a live TfL map for the nearest bus option; the cleanest planned fallback is to return towards Ruislip before you get too far north-west.

The Metropolitan line can still take you to a day that has no business feeling this unhurried.
