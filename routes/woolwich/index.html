Concrete, tidal water and enough horizon to clear a noisy head.

Begin in Woolwich, commit to the river and let the industrial scale build. The barrier is an anchor, not a photo stop to rush towards.

Best for.

  • A solo reset
  • Two people who genuinely like walking
  • Visitors curious about London’s edges

Not ideal for.

  • Low energy
  • Heavy rain or high wind
  • Anyone expecting frequent cafés

The route.

  1. Royal Arsenal Riverside

    start · 15–25 min · 40–50 min west to next

    Royal Arsenal Riverside, Woolwich, London SE18

    From the previous point: Arrive on the Elizabeth line and walk through the Royal Arsenal to the river. Sort coffee, water and the intended exit here.

    Parade-ground squares, repurposed munitions buildings and Dial Arch make the practical start; amenities get sparse after this.

    Walk here from the station (opens in a new tab)Open this point (opens in a new tab)

  2. The industrial river

    walk · 40–50 min · At the barrier to next

    Thames Path between Woolwich and Thames Barrier

    From the previous point: Follow only a currently open public Thames Path west. Aggregate wharves, working jetties and the wide sky are the point, not a failure of scenery.

    An exposed working-river stretch. Carry water and do not rely on frequent toilets or cafés.

    Walk from the previous stop (opens in a new tab)Open this point (opens in a new tab)

  3. Thames Barrier

    view · 20–35 min · 15–20 min west, or turn back to next

    Thames Barrier, Charlton, London SE7

    From the previous point: Approach from downstream so the barrier assembles itself as you walk. Check information-centre café hours before relying on it.

    The anchor and the clean decision point: continue if weather and daylight are good, or make this the good half-route and return to Woolwich.

    Walk from the previous stop (opens in a new tab)Open this point (opens in a new tab)

  4. Anchor & Hope, Charlton

    cafe-or-pub · 30–60 min · Finish to next

    Riverside, Charlton, London SE7

    From the previous point: Continue west on the riverside path to the pub. If it is closed, do not improvise: finish at the barrier and exit via Charlton or return to Woolwich.

    A practical, unpolished riverside finish. Confirm current hours before starting the long walk.

Finish and easy exit.

Anchor & Hope, Riverside Charlton

Nearest practical station: Charlton rail or Woolwich Elizabeth line

Charlton is roughly twelve minutes uphill from the pub. The honest half-route ends at the barrier café and returns to Woolwich.

Notes before you go.

Best time

Clear, dry daytime

Date notes

Not a first-date recommendation unless both people asked for a long industrial walk.

Friends

Two people is ideal; larger groups need more planning around pace and stops.

Solo

The primary use case, with normal daylight and safety judgement.

Food and drink

Bring water and a snack; food is not the route’s reliable feature.

When the plan changes.

If it rains

Do not force this in heavy rain or high wind; choose another route.

If it goes well

Complete the longer riverside section and finish near the second station.

If you want to leave early

Use the planned turn-back point or return to Woolwich before the exposed section continues.

What not to expect.

Not conventionally pretty, not short and not supported by constant amenities.

Practical warnings

  • Check path access, weather and daylight.
  • Carry water and do not rely on frequent toilets or cafés.
  • The prototype has not been field-checked.
