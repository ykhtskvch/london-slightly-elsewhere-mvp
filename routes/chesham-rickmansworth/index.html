Desk-checked from Chilterns National Landscape, TfL planning information and the pub’s current site in July 2026. The published route currently carries a flooding notice between Latimer and Chenies Bottom, so this is a prototype to check against live conditions — not a promise of dry feet.

The useful version

The rare kind of route where the Tube takes you out, then the water does the quiet work of the day.

What not to expect

Not a sealed, always-dry promenade or a spontaneous pub crawl. This is a real footpath day with live conditions to respect.
