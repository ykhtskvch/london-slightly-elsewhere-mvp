Books, old trees and a pub room without a great deal of ceremony.

Browse first, walk somewhere quiet, then finish in a verified pub. Each stage can be the natural ending, which is useful for first dates and low-energy days.

The route.

  1. Stoke Newington Church Street

    start · 5–10 min · 2–5 min to next

    Church Street, Stoke Newington, London N16

    From the previous point: Arrive by Stoke Newington rail or the 73 bus, then head for the bookshop at number 159.

    The street is the route’s spine: a clear start without needing an elaborate meeting point.

    Walk here from the station (opens in a new tab)Open this point (opens in a new tab)

  2. Stoke Newington Bookshop

    bookshop · 20–35 min · 3 min to next

    159 Church Street, London N16 0UH

    From the previous point: Continue along Church Street to number 159.

    A local independent browse first, so there is something to read in the pub later. Confirm current opening hours before making it the anchor.

    Walk from the previous stop (opens in a new tab)Open this point (opens in a new tab)

  3. Abney Park Cemetery

    walk · 25–45 min · 8 min, or skip to next

    Abney Park Cemetery, Stoke Newington High Street, London N16

    From the previous point: Walk to the cemetery entrance and use the signed paths inside. Check the closing time around dusk.

    Overgrown Victorian cemetery and nature reserve: the quiet pause, especially on a grey day. Skip it if the weather or company makes a cemetery wrong.

    Walk from the previous stop (opens in a new tab)Open this point (opens in a new tab)

  4. Clissold Park – optional long version

    walk · 20–40 min · Back to Church Street to next

    Clissold Park, London N16

    From the previous point: Continue only if the legs and light agree; otherwise return to Church Street after Abney Park.

    The optional wider-grass version: old church, deer enclosure and enough space to lengthen a good afternoon.

    Walk from the previous stop (opens in a new tab)Open this point (opens in a new tab)

  5. The Auld Shillelagh

    pub · 45–90 min · Finish to next

    105 Church Street, London N16 0AS

    From the previous point: Return to Church Street and head to number 105. If it is rammed, try The Rose and Crown or Jolly Butchers instead.

    The intended decent-pint finish: narrow, Irish and a natural point to stop. Verify current hours and seating before setting off.
