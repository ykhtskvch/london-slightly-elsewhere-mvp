Bloomsbury After Rain

Squares, books and somewhere warm: an indoor-outdoor route for days that should not be written off.

Russell Square Underground

Last walkedunverified — details not yet reconfirmed

A wet Tuesday in — honestly — the exact weather this route was written for. The Enlightenment Gallery was near-empty by 4pm. The bookshop's cake room had one table left, taken by someone reading, correctly. Russell Square's fountain was off, which somehow improved it.

The useful version

Wet stone, old squares and enough indoor pauses to make a rainy day feel intentional.

What not to expect

Not a grand museum day or a restaurant crawl; it is deliberately small-scale.
