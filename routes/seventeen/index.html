Unlisted

No names on this one.

No quick-facts, no filters, no easy exit. Just what to do, in order. Follow it from wherever you already are.

  1. Leave from wherever you already are. This one does not start at a station.
  2. Walk until the street stops feeling like a decision.
  3. Take the third street that smells of bread, or bins, or the river — whichever comes first.
  4. Cross the first thing that repeats: a bridge, a railway, a change underfoot from paving to something older.
  5. Stop at a doorway that looks like it has been open longer than you have.
  6. Buy one thing you did not come out to buy.
  7. Sit somewhere until the light changes.
  8. Go home a way you have not tried.

That is the whole route. It has no easy exit because leaving is always available — you just decided to keep going.
