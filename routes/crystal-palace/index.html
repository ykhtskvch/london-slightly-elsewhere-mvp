We never ask for your location.

A south London hill with just enough oddness to feel like a small trip.

Start with the climb, use the park as the strange middle chapter, then finish around the local high street rather than racing back to central London.

Who it suits, and who it does not.

  • A Sunday without plans
  • Friends who do not mind a hill
  • A local visitor day

Not ideal for

  • Very low walking energy
  • Heavy rain
  • Anyone demanding guaranteed seating

The hill is real, and the route is more peculiar than picturesque.

On a date

Better when both people like walking and a little oddness.

With friends

Good for a small group; agree about the hill before arrival.

Alone

A useful solo reset if you want distance and visual change.

The route.

  1. Crystal Palace station → park

    start · 10 min · 10 min to next

    Crystal Palace station, London SE19

    From the previous point: Use the station exit nearest the park and head directly to the lower lake.

    The park entrance is immediate; start low and save the hill for later.

    Walk here from the station (opens in a new tab)Open this point (opens in a new tab)

  2. Crystal Palace Dinosaurs

    walk · 10–20 min · 20–25 min uphill to next

    Lower lake, Crystal Palace Park, London SE19

    From the previous point: Follow the park paths to the lower lake and dinosaur sculptures.

    The 1854 dinosaur sculptures are confidently and magnificently wrong about anatomy: five minutes with a good story built in.

    Walk from the previous stop (opens in a new tab)Open this point (opens in a new tab)

  3. Terraces, ruins and transmitter view

    view · 20–30 min · 5 min to next

    Crystal Palace Park terraces, London SE19

    From the previous point: Continue uphill through the terraces. The climb is real; pause at the ruins and view rather than treating it as a march.

    Broken staircases, sphinxes and the Palace footprint, with the transmitter tower doing its best Eiffel impression.

    Walk from the previous stop (opens in a new tab)Open this point (opens in a new tab)

  4. The Triangle – Westow Hill / Church Road

    walk · 20–40 min · 2–5 min to next

    Westow Hill, Crystal Palace, London SE19

    From the previous point: Exit the park from the upper corner and walk to Westow Hill. Browse only if the energy is there; shop hours need checking.

    The browsing and lamp-argument stretch before the pub: make it a short local high-street chapter, not another obligation.

    Walk from the previous stop (opens in a new tab)Open this point (opens in a new tab)

  5. Westow House

    pub · 45–90 min · Finish to next

    79 Westow Hill, London SE19 1TX

    From the previous point: Continue along Westow Hill to number 79. The quieter fallback is The White Hart on Church Road.

    A big, lively local finish that matches the route’s livelier tag; verify current hours before setting off.

    Walk from the previous stop (opens in a new tab)Open this point (opens in a new tab)

Finish and easy exit.

Westow House, 79 Westow Hill

Nearest practical station: Crystal Palace Overground or Gipsy Hill

Crystal Palace is downhill from the Triangle; Gipsy Hill is the alternative from the other side. Confirm your preferred exit in Maps before the final drink.
